# How Glass Bottles Are Made: Flow prompts (Nano Banana 2.1, 16:9, x1, download 2K)

## Visual bible

**STYLE (photo shots):** Editorial mixed-media collage on layered torn paper, masking tape, pins and hand-drawn pencil arrows, photographic subject cut out with a clean paper edge, warm cream and kraft tones with muted green accents, overhead soft daylight. Paper tags are blank.

**STYLE (cutaways):** Editorial mixed-media collage on layered torn paper, masking tape and pins, a precise technical cutaway drawing in pencil and watercolour pasted on cream paper, warm cream and kraft tones with muted green accents. Paper tags are blank.

**SUFFIX (every prompt):** Wide 16:9 composition. The main subject is centered and every important detail sits inside the middle third of the frame; the left and right thirds contain only continuous background (environment, texture, soft out-of-focus surroundings). No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks or borders.

**ACCENT:** `#E4572E`

**ROSTER**

- BOTTLE: one clear glass bottle, tall slim narrow neck, gently sloping shoulders, smooth straight sides, plain crown lip, no label, no cap. Reference: `assets/refs/ref-bottle-test.jpg`.
- MOULD: heavy two-part steel mould, dark machined metal.

**Heat colours:** melt yellow-white, gob yellow-orange, parison orange, finished bottle very dim dark red.

## Prompts

Each line is the subject. Add the matching STYLE, then SUFFIX.
