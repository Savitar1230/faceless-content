Never feed a calf from a low bucket. A calf has a hidden switch in its throat that decides where the milk goes.
Head up, sucking like it's nursing, the switch snaps shut. The milk skips the first stomach and goes straight to digestion.
Head down at a bucket, the switch can stay open. The milk spills into the first stomach, goes sour, and the calf bloats.
So the teats sit at nursing height, and the milk goes in warm. Cold milk upsets the gut and brings on scours.
